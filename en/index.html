Frequently asked questions

Where does my data stay?

Your data stays in your hands: with local AI it never leaves your device, with your own server it is stored exclusively on servers in Germany. In hybrid mode only pseudonymised content leaves your company.

How does local AI work?

The AI model runs directly on a device inside your company. Documents are processed on-site – with no per-request billing and without content being transferred to third parties.

Which documents can the AI read?

Common office formats such as PDF, Word, Excel, e-mails and text files. Answers include source references so you can verify every statement.

Is the system GDPR-compliant?

EIGENKERN is designed around GDPR requirements: data processing in Germany, pseudonymisation by the Guardian and full control over storage locations. We are happy to advise you personally on your specific use case.
